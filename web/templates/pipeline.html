Step 1

Find NASA papers

Searches NASA's report server (NTRS) for papers on an experiment and adds them.

Step 2

Have the AI read them

Pulls test conditions and findings out of papers that are still waiting. Each paper takes 5–30 seconds.

To extract one paper, open it in Literature.

Step 3

Check the AI's work

Open any paper in Literature to verify, reject or correct each extracted condition and finding. Rejected rows drop out of the Explorer and Compare totals.

Also

Import a Google Sheet

Adds experiments and NTRS citations from a shared sheet. The sheet must be viewable by link.
