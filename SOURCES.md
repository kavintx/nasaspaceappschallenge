# Sources

Every result in the site's "Tests from other sources" layer, grouped by publisher. This list is generated from
`web/data/more_sources.json`, where each row also carries the page it was read from and the exact quote.

**533 results** for **99 materials**, read from **189 documents** by **87 publishers**.
By gravity: 490 Earth, 35 microgravity, 5 lunar gravity, 3 Martian gravity.

Accepted sources are government agencies and laboratories, standards bodies, university repositories, peer-reviewed
journals, and a named manufacturer's own data sheet. Wikipedia, forums, blogs and pages that do not say who measured
the value are not used. NASA's microgravity experiments themselves are in the research database, not in this list;
the site links each of those tests to its paper on the NASA Technical Reports Server.
